**Why HiveCraic?**

As fellow beekeepers, we know the challenges of keeping accurate records while managing busy apiaries. HiveCraic was built to make this easier, with features that directly benefit Irish beekeepers:

**🐝 Protecting the Native Irish Honey Bee**
By using HiveCraic, you contribute to a growing dataset on Irish beekeeping. This anonymised data helps us better understand colony health patterns, regional challenges, and the factors affecting our native *Apis mellifera mellifera*. Together, we can build evidence-based insights that support conservation efforts and improve beekeeping practices across Ireland.

**🗺️ Community Map**
HiveCraic features a **community map** where beekeepers can discover other HiveCraic users in their area. This makes it easier to connect with fellow beekeepers, find local mentors, coordinate on swarm collection, or simply know who your beekeeping neighbours are. It's a great way to strengthen the local beekeeping community.

**🍯 Marketing & Traceability Tools**
Each beekeeper can generate a **public traceability page** for their honey. Customers simply scan a QR code to see exactly where their honey comes from - the apiary location, harvest date, and batch information. This transparency builds trust and adds real value to your product, helping you stand out at markets and farm shops.

**📋 Compliance Made Simple**
HiveCraic makes it straightforward to maintain the records required under Irish regulations, including detailed logs of varroa treatments, medications used, and application dates. Having these records organised and accessible helps beekeepers stay compliant with DAFM requirements and prepares you for any inspections.

**Key Features:**
- Apiary and hive management
- Queen tracking and breeding records
- Inspection logging
- Honey harvest and batch tracking
- Shared apiary management - let your club members collaborate on hive records
- Community map to connect with local beekeepers

# Plan: Dashboard Brief (facts, suggestions and visit planning)

**Date:** 30 October 2026 · **Status:** Phase 1 built, not yet tested in a browser or committed. Phases 2 and 3 not started

The full plan, with mock-ups, wording rules and to-do lists, is in [`tasks/dashboard-brief-todo.html`](../../tasks/dashboard-brief-todo.html).

## Goal

The dashboard says what to do next and why, and never presents a missing record as a problem.

## Scope

- **Phase 1, trust and layout (no new tables):** reconcile job counts, label each job Recorded, Scale, Task or Suggested, fix misleading wording, give each seasonal row its own link, move recording actions to the top, group recent activity into visits, and show the source of the monthly guidance.
- **Phase 2, visit planning:** add jobs and open tasks to the apiary page, and link to the existing Visit Checklist rather than building a second one.
- **Phase 3, only if earned:** queen follow-ups you confirm, stale scale readings, and testing with club members.

## Left out

Sync status (the app has no offline saving), snooze and dismiss, experience levels, a widget builder, health scores and AI summaries.

## Owner's decisions (30 October 2026)

1. Keep the seasonal card, in a shorter form.
2. Narrow the queen check to hives actually inspected in the last 21 days, or recorded as broodless.
3. Correct the about page's offline claim.

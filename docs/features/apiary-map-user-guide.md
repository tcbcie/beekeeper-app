# Apiary map: a guide for beekeepers

The apiary map shows where each hive stands in an apiary, drawn to scale. Open an apiary and choose **Apiary Map**, or open a hive and choose **Show on map**.

The map needs an active subscription. It can be switched off in Profile under "Apiary map".

## Looking at the map

- The whole apiary fits the screen. Use **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit** to enlarge it. A zoomed map scrolls sideways.
- **Tap a hive** to see its queen, last inspection, stores, tasks and treatments. From there you can **Start inspection** or **Open hive**.
- **Show** changes what the colours mean:
  - **Queens**: green has a queen, amber has no queen recorded, red is queenless.
  - **Last inspection**: green in the last week, amber due soon, red overdue (over 14 days). Grey from November to February.
  - **Winter stores**: green enough, amber top up, red low, grey not checked this season. Not offered from May to July.
  - **Tasks and treatments**: amber has open tasks or a treatment on, red has a treatment overdue for removal.
- Amber hives carry a "!" and red hives a "!!", so the colours are not the only signal. The legend under the map explains the current view.
- The orange triangle on a hive marks its entrance side.
- **Print** prints the map with the legend and a list of the hives.
- **3D view** shows the same layout in 3D.

## Arranging the map

Press **Arrange**. Press **Done** when you have finished. Every change is saved as you make it.

- **Move a hive**: press and hold it, then drag. Or tap it, choose **Move**, and tap the new spot.
- **Place a hive that is not on the map yet**: drag it from the list under the map, or tap it and choose **Place on map**.
- **Turn a hive**: tap it and use the turn buttons, or drag the round handle above it.
- **Take a hive off the map**: tap it and choose **Remove from map**.
- **Undo**: after a move, the message at the bottom of the screen offers **Undo**.

Under **Map setup** the apiary owner can:

- set the **entrance** (tap the button, then tap the map),
- **add a bench** for 1 to 6 hives; a hive dropped on a bench takes a place on it and moves with it,
- **add a landmark**: a shed, tree, water, gate or a text label,
- change the **size** of the apiary in metres,
- turn **north**.

## Who can change what

- The **apiary owner** can change everything.
- **Team members** of a shared apiary can move and turn hives.
- Everyone else can look and print.

## Without a signal

The map you last saw is kept on your phone and is shown when there is no signal, with a note saying so. It cannot be arranged until the signal is back.

## Good to know

- A hive moved to another apiary leaves the map and must be placed again on the new one.
- Archived hives are not shown.
- You can ask Mel "Where is hive 12?" or "Which hive is next to hive 3?".

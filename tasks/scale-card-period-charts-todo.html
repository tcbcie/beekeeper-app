Scale Card: Chart per Reporting Period

Status: plan, awaiting approval  ·  Date: 29 October 2026

Your instruction: for the reporting periods (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days) on the scale card, make individual charts available that pop out on request.

1. What already exists

  • BEEP chart: src/components/hive/ScaleHistoryChart.tsx — weight and brood temperature over time, with Hour / Day / Week / Month / Year / Custom buttons. Fetches its own data. Used on the hive page.
  • Wolf chart: src/components/hive/WolfScalePanel.tsx — the same chart plus stat tiles for the chosen period. Fetches its own data. Used on the hive page.
  • Period mapping: the data routes know day = last 24 hours, week = last 7 days, month = last 30 days — exactly the card’s three columns.
  • Dialog: src/components/ui/ModalShell.tsx (Escape to close, focus kept inside, 48px close button).

So no new chart is needed: the pop-out shows the hive page’s own chart, opened on the period that was tapped. The one thing missing is a way to tell each chart which period to start on.

Details

  • Card (HiveScaleCard.tsx): each period gets its chart period (day/week/month); its column heading becomes a button with a chart icon and underline, aria-label e.g. “Show the 7-day weight chart”. Tapping opens a ModalShell (wide on desktop, scrolls on a phone).
  • Charts: one optional prop initialPeriod on ScaleHistoryChart.tsx and WolfScalePanel.tsx. Default stays as today (Day / Week), so the hive page is unchanged.
  • Loaded on demand with next/dynamic: nothing chart-related loads until a heading is tapped, and each tap costs one scale request (Wolf: three on first open, for battery, as on the hive page).
  • Wolf note in the pop-out: Wolf’s 7- and 30-day figures on the card leave out feeding and harvest jumps, but the chart draws the raw weight, so the chart’s start-to-end difference can differ from the number tapped. A one-line note under the Wolf chart says so, so nobody thinks one of them is wrong.

QA audit (qa-engineer)

  • No Critical, High or Medium findings. npx tsc --noEmit: no errors. ESLint on the three files: clean.
  • The dialog is rendered inside the card, which has overflow-hidden. A fixed overlay escapes that unless an ancestor has a transform or filter; none of the card’s ancestors (Scale Overview tab, Research page, dashboard layout) has one, so the dialog covers the screen.
  • chartPeriod state sits with the other hooks, above the loading and error early returns, so hook order is stable.
  • The chart components fetch their own data with their existing sequence guard; the card’s 5-minute polling is unaffected. “Refresh All” remounts the cards and so closes an open chart, which is expected.
  • ⚪ Low, left as is: the charts keep Chart.js’s 12px text and their own dark-mode colours (see section 4).

Proposal: Simpler Dashboard That Tells You What to Do

Status: Step A and Step B implemented, awaiting your test  ·  Date: 29 October 2026

Your instruction: the dashboard looks overloaded. Suggest how to simplify it, but add value.

1. Why it feels overloaded

  1. The same facts four times. Your four apiaries are a few kilometres apart, so every card repeats the same weather, “3366 GDD”, the same forage chips (Ivy, Erica, Daisies), “Nectar: Good”, “Pollen: Good” and nearly the same foraging window. That is two-thirds of each card.
  2. Cryptic lines. “Foraging window: (-1d) 0.8h · Today 2.8h · (+1d) 0.3h” and “Avg (2) Today -0.51” need decoding.
  3. The cards describe, they don’t direct. “Last inspected 13d ago” is the most recently inspected hive, so an apiary can show “4d ago” while another hive there has not been seen for a month. The real per-hive warnings (overdue inspections, high varroa) are worked out already but only shown as totals at the top of the page.
  4. Tasks in four places (alert chip, card pill, stats strip, Upcoming events).

Two bugs found on the way

  • “6 tasks” can open a list showing fewer.
  • The pill counts every open task, including future ones, but the Tasks page opens on “Due” (start date today or earlier). ApiaryWeatherRow.tsx:897-907, tasks/page.tsx:119.
  • The loading skeleton still draws 6 quick-action placeholders; the page has 2 (dashboard/page.tsx:208-236).

3. The changes, in two steps

Step A — simplify (no database change)

#ChangeValue
A1One “Today around your apiaries” strip above the cards: weather, 7-day forecast, flying hours, nectar/pollen, GDD and what is in bloom. Taken from the nearest apiary. The cards drop those rows. If an apiary is more than 20 km from the others it keeps its own forage line, so nothing is hidden where conditions really differ.Each card loses about two-thirds of its height; the page is roughly half as long on a phone.
A2Plain wording: “2.8 h flying weather today (tomorrow 0.3 h)”; scale row “Scale weight change (kg): today / 7 days / 30 days”, matching the Scale Overview card.Nothing to decode.
A3Fix the task pill: count and link agree (the pill opens the list showing all of that apiary’s open tasks).Bug fix.
A448px tap areas for the 7-day toggle, bloom chips and task pill; skeleton shows 2 quick actions.Easier with gloves; no jump on load.

Step B — add value (one database function change, via the MCP server)

#ChangeValue
B1“Needs attention” line per apiary: hives due for inspection (14+ days), queen issues, high varroa (>3% in 30 days), overdue tasks. Green “All good” when there is nothing. The dashboard function already works these out for the whole account; it would also return them per apiary.Answers “where do I go today, and why?” at a glance.
B2“Oldest check” instead of “Last inspected”: days since the least recently inspected hive.Stops a recent visit hiding a neglected hive.
B3Cards that need attention first, then nearest first (as now).The apiary to visit is at the top.

Not proposed

  • Removing the stats strip, Recent activity or Upcoming events — they sit below the apiaries and are already compact. I would revisit them after Step A.
  • Swarm risk or treatment-strip reminders on the card — possible later, but not computed for the dashboard today.

Profit & Loss – income and expenses chart

Follows the category breakdowns (commits 620fed04 and 6e187abf). Feature doc: docs/features/profit-loss-tracker.md.

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • A bar chart, Income and expenses by month, sits between the totals and the category panels. Each month has two bars side by side: income in green and expenses in red. There is a legend at the top, and tapping a bar shows "Income: €120.00".
  • It follows the time filter (your choice, see the question):
    • This Year: January to December of this year. Months still to come are empty, so the season always reads the same way.
    • All Time: one pair of bars for each year that has records, "Income and expenses by year". Months across several years would be too thin to read on a phone.
    • This Month: no chart, because one month is already shown by the totals.
  • It is hidden when the period has no records.

Design checks (dataviz skill)

  • One axis in euro or pound, the same unit for both series. No second axis.
  • Colours checked with the validator script, not by eye. Plain green-500 and red-500, as on the cards, fail for colour-blind readers (ΔE 3.5), so the chart uses darker steps that are all existing Tailwind colours:
    ModeIncomeExpensesResult
    Light (on white)green-700 #15803dred-600 #dc2626All checks pass, CVD ΔE 8.6
    Dark (on #111827)green-600 #16a34ared-700 #b91c1cAll checks pass, CVD ΔE 11.0
  • The legend is always shown, and income is always the left bar, so identity never rests on colour alone. Bars have 4px rounded tops sitting on the baseline, with a small gap between the pair.
  • Text is 14px and the grid is recessive, both through themeChartOptions. isDarkMode() is in the useMemo dependencies.
  • Screen readers get a short summary on the chart, plus a hidden table of the same figures (month, income, expenses).

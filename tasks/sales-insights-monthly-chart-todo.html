Sales Insights – monthly income chart

Follows the Sales Insights year selector (commit 392b4ea5) and the P&L chart (MonthlyChart.tsx). Feature doc: docs/features/crm-module.md.

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • A chart, Income by month (2025), sits under the headline cards. It shows January to December of the year chosen in the Year drop-down.
  • Each month is one stacked bar: "From orders" at the bottom and "Other" (jar sales and income added by hand) on top. The full height is that month's income. This is the same split as the "Income in {year}" card, now month by month, so the season shape and where the money came from read together.
  • Tapping anywhere in a month shows "From orders €X", "Other €Y" and "Total €Z".
  • It is hidden when the chosen year has no income.

Design checks (dataviz skill, validator run)

  • Two series, one money axis. The bars are stacked because the two parts add up to a meaningful total, the month's income.
  • "From orders" and "Other" are two kinds of income, not good and bad, so they need two clearly different hues. Results:
    OptionLightDark (on #111827)
    A. Green and blue#15803d and #2563eb: every check passes, ΔE 27.4#16a34a and #3b82f6: every check passes, ΔE 27.5
    B. Green and honey amber#166534 and #d97706: every check passes, ΔE 14.7#16a34a and #d97706: ΔE 6.2, the warning band, allowed only with a second cue (fixed order and a gap between segments)
  • "From orders" is always at the bottom, with a 2px gap between the segments and 4px rounded tops. The legend is always shown.
  • Text is 14px through themeChartOptions and in the tooltips. Screen readers get a summary and a hidden table (month, from orders, other, total).

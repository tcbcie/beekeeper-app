Sales Insights chart – year comparison

Matches the P&L year comparison (tasks/pl-year-comparison-todo.html, commit 613e0167). Feature doc: docs/features/crm-module.md (Sales Insights).

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • A Compare with drop-down sits next to Year. It starts at "None" and lists the other years with income or orders, newest first. It is the same control as on P&L.
  • When a year is chosen, the Income by month chart adds a dashed grey line, "Income 2025". It shows that year's total income for each month, so it lines up with the top of each stacked bar. A bar above the line means a better month than last year.
  • If the comparison year is the current year, its line stops at this month. For a past year it runs all twelve months, which also shows the shape of the season still to come when you view this year.
  • Tapping a month shows From orders, Other, Total and "Income 2025".
  • The "Income in 2026" card gains a line "2025: €X" under its split. Outstanding and Open to fulfil stay as today's position. Income by category and Top customers stay as the chosen year.
  • The comparison switches itself off when it would equal the chosen year.

Design checks (dataviz skill)

  • It uses the same reference mark as P&L: a neutral grey (light #6b7280, dark #9ca3af, at least 4.5:1), 2px with 4px dashes and 8px hollow points. It is told apart from the green and blue bars by its form (a line), its dashes and the legend. The validated green and blue are unchanged.
  • The line has its own stack, so it is never added on top of the stacked bars. One money axis.
  • The hidden table gains an "Income 2025" column, and the summary names the comparison.

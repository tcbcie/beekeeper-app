Profit & Loss chart – year comparison

Follows the P&L year selector (commit 45e71f42) and the net profit line (commit 55af9cc1). Feature doc: docs/features/profit-loss-tracker.md.

The question a beekeeper is asking

"Am I doing better or worse than last year, and in which months?" The chart can show one year at a time. All Time already compares whole years side by side, but not month by month.

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • With Year chosen, a second drop-down, Compare with, sits next to Year. It starts at "None" and lists the other years that have records, newest first.
  • When a year is chosen, the chart adds a dashed grey line, "Net profit 2025", month by month, next to this year's solid blue net line. Above the lines means a better month than last year. If the comparison year is the current year, its line stops at this month, as usual.
  • Tapping a month shows this year's income, expenses and net profit, plus "Net profit 2025".
  • The three totals cards each gain a small line under the figure, for example "2025: €2,140", so the whole-year comparison is there without reading the chart.
  • "Compare with" is cleared when you choose the same year in Year, or leave the Year period.

Design checks (dataviz skill)

  • The comparison is one extra line, not six series. Last year's income and expenses bars as well would make 12 months × 4 bars, too thin to read on a phone. Net profit is the one figure that sums up a month.
  • Grey and dashed is a reference mark, not a new category. Last year in neutral grey is the usual way to show "previous period". It is told apart from this year's blue by both its dash and its colour, and by the legend. Grey (light #6b7280, dark #9ca3af) has at least 4.5:1 contrast on both surfaces. The validator's colour-variety check does not apply to a neutral reference line. The existing three colours are unchanged.
  • The line is 2px with 4px dashes and 8px hollow points, so it stays visible where it crosses the blue line. One money axis, as before.
  • The hidden table for screen readers gains a "Net profit 2025" column, and the summary says what is compared.

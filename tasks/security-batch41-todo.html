Batch 41: Research Data

Status: Implemented 24/10/2026, waiting for your test. This is Phase 2 fix batch 28. It covers the logic and data findings in chunk 7b (research, GDD, wild colonies, the public embed): C7b-06, 07, 08, 10 to 18, 21, 22. Batch 42 will take the screen findings (C7b-23 to C7b-25: tap sizes, chart text and dark mode, the wide wild-colonies table). Three agents checked each finding against the code and the live database, and I re-checked the key lines.

A. GDD charts: C7b-06, C7b-08, C7b-18

C7b-18 and C7b-06, old lines stay after clearing the years: untick every year on the GDD accumulation chart (dashboard or the tcbc.ie embed) and the old lines stay, sometimes appearing after you've cleared them. The "Select at least one year" message never shows. Now the chart clears. The Foraging Hours chart has the same flaw and gets the same fix.
C7b-08: the GDD tab sorts its list of selected years in place while drawing, which can stop the screen updating. It now sorts a copy, as the embed already does.
The other "no stale guard" cases are already handled (batch 24 added guards) or can't show wrong data.

B. Wild colonies: C7b-10 to C7b-13

C7b-10, coordinates not checked: latitude and longitude are free text. "abc" gives a raw database error, "53.27x" is quietly saved as 53.27, and 532744 is saved as it is and then sent to the weather service. Now both must be real numbers within ±90 / ±180, or you get a clear message.
C7b-11, temperature can't be left blank: in a wild colony inspection, clearing the temperature fetches the weather again and refills it. Typing a minus sign does the same, so you can't enter a negative temperature. Now the weather fills the field once, only while it's empty.
C7b-12, 0°C: an inspection at 0°C shows a blank field, hides its weather line, or shows a stray "0". The scale card shows "-0°C" for -0.4. All fixed.
C7b-13: the inspection form sets the photo preview during drawing, which React warns about and which ignores a changed photo. It now does it properly. The form also gets a key, so opening another inspection doesn't keep the old fields.

C. Research page and scale cards: C7b-14, C7b-15, C7b-16

C7b-14, blank or stuck research page: a link to a section you don't have access to (diagnosis images, wild colonies) shows no tab and no content. A rare sign-in error leaves the spinner up for good. Now it falls back to the GDD section, and the spinner always ends.
C7b-15, repeated calls: on Research → Scale Overview, every hive card fetches its own weather and GDD every 30 minutes, even for hives in the same apiary. Its scale readings refresh every 5 minutes, even while the app is in the background. Now:
  • Weather and GDD are fetched once per location.
  • Refreshing pauses while the tab is hidden. The same applies to the scale readings on the hive pages.
  • The dashboard weather row shares one weather request between cards with the same location.
C7b-16, GDD formula copied five times: the GDD tab, the embed and the scale card each carry their own copy. The copies read the month in a way that shifts by a day west of Ireland. They now use the shared formula. The tcbc.ie widget script keeps its own copy, as it must, with its "keep in sync" note.

D. Public embed: C7b-07

C7b-07, every tcbc.ie visitor calls the weather service: the research embed makes 2–6 weather-archive calls from each visitor's browser. On a busy day that heads towards the free usage limits. The free tier is also for non-commercial use, and HiveCraic takes subscriptions. Now:
  • The embed asks our own server.
  • The server fetches each region and year once and keeps it for 6 hours (30 days for past years), so the weather service sees a handful of calls whatever the traffic.
  • The server only accepts the listed regions and sensible years, so it can't be used as an open relay.
  • The dashboard's own GDD calls are unchanged.

E. Data reads: C7b-21, C7b-22

C7b-21, diagnosis list: it loads in one request, which stops at 1,000 rows. There are only 2 today, but power users see everyone's. It now reads in pages.
C7b-22, shared GDD records sent nationwide: the database sends every shared bloom record in the country (76 today), with each owner's user ID, and the browser then keeps those within 20 km of your apiaries. Now the database does the filtering: it leaves out your own records, returns only those within 20 km of one of your apiaries, and no longer sends user IDs. The locations stay blurred, as before.
Database change: one migration, applied through MCP and saved in supabase/migrations/. It replaces get_shared_gdd_records under the same name, so nothing that calls it changes. Signed-in users only, as now, with the security advisors checked afterwards.

F. Closed without a change

C7b-17, the embed is a near copy of the GDD tab: they differ in data source, filters, sharing and events, so merging would need a shared component with half a dozen switches, on a page live on tcbc.ie. Pointing both at the shared formula and fixing them together (above) removes the real risk.

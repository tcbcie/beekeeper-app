Batch 36: Hive Detail and Records

Status: Implemented 24/10/2026, waiting for your test. This is Phase 2 fix batch 23. It covers the remaining Medium findings in hives and scales (C3-08, C3-09, C3-10, C3-12, C3-18, C3-19, C3-20) and in records, tasks and the logbook (C4-13, C4-14, C4-15, C4-17, C4-18, C4-19). Three agents checked each finding against the code and the live database, and I re-checked the key facts. No database change is needed.

A. Hive detail page: C3-08, C3-09

C3-09, the page blanks on every refresh: after you pick a scale (or unarchive a hive), the whole page turns into a spinner and comes back at the top. The scale chart resets to its first range, and everything below reloads. Now the spinner only shows on the first load; a refresh updates the page in place.
C3-08, an older answer can win: once the page stays on screen during a refresh, two quick refreshes could finish in the wrong order and show the older data. Each load is now numbered, and only the latest one updates the page, using the same pattern as the hives list.

B. Scale charts: C3-12

Switching Day → Month → Day quickly can leave Month's data under the "Day" button, because the slowest answer arrives last. The BEEP and Wolf charts now only show the answer to the latest request. A custom range is only fetched when both dates are filled in and the start is not after the end.

C. QR tags: C3-10, C3-18, C3-19, C3-20

C3-18, printed codes can point at the wrong site: QR codes are built from the address the app is open on. A tag printed from a test or preview address keeps that address for ever. The hive and nuc QR codes, the QR tags page and the public jar and batch labels now always use https://www.hivecraic.com, from one new constant. Sign-in links still use the current address, which is correct for them.
C3-20, wrong message when you can't see the hive: scanning a tag whose hive you no longer have access to says "This tag is not yet assigned", and offers "Manage QR Tags". Now it says the tag is on a hive or nuc you don't have access to, and to ask its owner to share the apiary. "Tag not found" also mentions access.
C3-19, a button inside a link: the "Open hive" button on the apiary map's hive panel sits inside a link, which is invalid and gives screen readers and keyboards two stops. It becomes one link with the same look and size. The same pattern on the queens and orders pages belongs to other chunks and is left for their batches.
C3-10, QR order: the hive page and the records list sort tags newest first, but an undated tag would sort to the top. No live tag is undated, so nothing is wrong today; both sorts now put undated tags last, as the hives list already does.

D. Records: C4-13, C4-14, C4-15, C4-17

C4-13, the microphone can stay on: if you close the inspection while the browser is still asking for microphone permission, and then allow it, recording starts with nothing to stop it. A double tap on "Record voice note" does the same. The recorder now stops a microphone that arrives after it was cancelled, and ignores a second tap while one is starting.
C4-14, a wasted weather call: every hive you pick in the inspection form fetches the weather, and the result is thrown away. The weather that is saved is fetched again when you save. That's 2 to 4 wasted requests per pick, even when editing. The unused call is removed.
C4-15, ignored errors: on the records page, if loading your hive list fails (for example on a poor signal), every records list is emptied without a word. To you, it looks like your records have vanished. Now the lists are left as they were and you get a "Couldn't load your records" warning. A failed refresh of one list also keeps it instead of leaving it half-updated, and the hive and apiary pickers keep their contents. Unnamed embeds: not real today; I checked the live foreign keys and none of these embeds is ambiguous.
C4-17, sign-off removed with one tap: "Remove" on a logbook assessor sign-off deletes it at once, and it can't be recreated without the assessor. It now asks first, using the app's usual confirm dialog.

E. Sizes for 50+ eyes: C4-18, C4-19

C4-18, 7 fields with 14px text: the records date filters and the logbook sign-off form. They become 16px.
C4-19, 61 tap targets under 48px across the record forms, the records filters, the inspection card, the tasks form and the logbook. Most are inputs at about 42px, or 44px classes, some of which cap a button that would otherwise be 48px. Each gets min-h-12 (48px). Eight inspection buttons that shrink to 40px on tablets keep 48px. The two remove-photo buttons keep their small red circle inside a 48px tap area, as in batch 30.
This is a class-only sweep, with no layout or behaviour changes. Labels and button text keep their size; the 16px rule covers the fields you type in.

Finishing

Type-check, lint and the whole test suite; a short test for the voice recorder's cancelled start; the qa-engineer audit.
Docs: docs/features/hive-detail-records-batch36-plan.md, the Phase 2 register and memory if a new rule comes out of it.
You:
  1. On a hive with a scale, change the scale range quickly a few times: the chart matches the button. Assign a scale: the page stays where you were, with no full-page spinner.
  2. Open a hive's QR code: it points at www.hivecraic.com.
  3. Start a voice note in an inspection and close the form: the browser's microphone indicator goes off.
  4. In the logbook, tap "Remove" on a sign-off: it asks first.
  5. Look over the inspection, feeding, harvest and varroa forms on your phone: fields and buttons are the full size.

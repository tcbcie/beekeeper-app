WP03 – Make state and task wording truthful

Plan: docs/features/mobile-first-implementation-plan.html (WP03, its refinements and owner decision 1). Findings: F06, F12, D01, I01, R02, V18–V20. Mostly wording; two small behaviour changes (B1, B2). No database change.

A. Wording

  1. Winter label (D01, decided): src/lib/seasonal-lead.ts "Winter: nothing to do" becomes "Winter: just check food"; the same in docs/features/dashboard-today-screen.md.
  2. Review step (I01), InspectionForm.tsx:
    • New: "Check the details below, then save. The inspection is not saved until you do."
    • Edit: "Check your changes below, then tap Update Inspection. They are not saved until you do."
    • "Only the visit details have been recorded." becomes "Only the visit details have been filled in." so "recorded" no longer means two things.
    • Update the one existing test that checks the old sentence (inspection-stepped-flow.test.tsx).
  3. Log in / sign up (R02), src/app/login/page.tsx: the description follows the mode ("Create an account to track…" when signing up), and the green main button follows the mode (Sign Up is green when creating an account, Login otherwise). Enter already works, so it is untouched.
  4. Outbox wording (F06, V20). Waiting can mean no signal, an expired session, a server problem or another tab sending, and sending only happens while the app is open. So:
    • List heading "Saved on this phone, not sent yet" becomes "Saved on this device, not sent yet".
    • Each waiting row: "Sends by itself when there is signal" becomes "Sends when the app is open and has signal".
    • Send now, still waiting: "Still no signal. It will send by itself when there is." becomes "Could not send it just now. It will try again when the app is next open with signal."
    • After a save: "Saved on this phone. It will send by itself when there is signal." becomes "Saved on this device. It will send when the app is open and has signal."
    • Offline banner: "New inspections stay on this phone and send when you reconnect." becomes "New inspections stay on this device and send when the app is open and back online."
  5. "Phone" on a desktop: the other user-facing "this phone" strings become "this device". These are the Carry on panel, the Unfinished inspection notice, the outbox Discard question, the log-out warning (useLogout.ts), and the two draft lines added in WP02.
  6. Reset page backticks (V18), forgot-password/page.tsx: "The reset link will open `Reset Password`." becomes "The reset link opens the Reset Password page."

B. Small behaviour changes

  1. Warnings after an inspection is sent stay until dismissed ("follow-up tasks could not be created", "the super count may not have updated"). Today they vanish after 4 seconds. They become toasts with no time limit (toast.warning(message, 0)), so they stay on screen until closed. They do not survive a reload. Four call sites: two in records/page.tsx, two in InspectionOutbox.tsx.
  2. Super count not changed, and no warning (V19). src/lib/inspection-outbox.ts: the RPC returns nothing when it cannot see the hive. That now gives the warning "Inspection saved. The super count on hive X was not updated, so please check it."

  • inspection-drafts.md lines 10 and 59: now refer to the outbox as built, not "Phase G" as future work.

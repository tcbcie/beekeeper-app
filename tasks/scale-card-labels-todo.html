Two data bugs found on the way (root causes)

  • Wolf humidity never shows. The Wolf data route sends humidity_percent; the card reads lv?.humidity (HiveScaleCard.tsx:164), which is always empty. Fix: read humidity_percent.
  • The flame temperature is not the same thing on both scales. Wolf sends a brood-nest sensor (brood_temp_c). BEEP sends its inside temperature (t_i ?? t ?? t_0), measured by the scale base, which is why your BEEP card shows 13.8°C. Today both are called “Brood temperature”. Fix: label Wolf “Brood temp.” and BEEP “Inside temp.”.
  • GDD can show a decimal (the helper rounds to 0.1). It will be shown as a whole number, as Wolf does.

Details

  • Header: hive number on its own line; the scale serial moves to a second line prefixed “Scale”, so the double brackets go. Wolf/BEEP pill, refresh and “View hive” stay. The refresh button grows to a 48px tap target.
  • Weather row: prefixed “Outside now:” so it is not mistaken for scale readings; apiary gets a location pin.
  • Weight: larger, with “Total weight” under it.
  • Changes: a 3-column table — columns “24 hours / 7 days / 30 days”, rows “This period” and “Period before”, unit in the corner label “Change (kg)”. Arrows stay (so it does not rely on colour alone); green/red get dark-mode variants and stronger shades for contrast.
  • Readings: labelled tiles — “Brood temp.” (Wolf) or “Inside temp.” (BEEP), “Battery” (red when under 20%, as now), “GDD since 1 Jan” (tooltip: “Growing degree days since 1 January – Wolf calls this GTS”), “Scale humidity” when the scale reports it (tooltip: measured by the scale, not the weather forecast).
  • Text: nothing under 14px, sentence-case labels, tabular-nums so figures line up. Tooltips stay as a bonus, but nothing depends on hovering.
  • No new data or requests — same fetches, same values; only src/components/research/HiveScaleCard.tsx changes.

Delete a supplier

Status: built, waiting for your tests  ·  Date: 15 November 2026  ·  Feature doc: docs/features/profit-loss-suppliers.md (extended)

Your request: add an option to delete a supplier.

This follows on from suppliers on expenses (2e84b3e6); that plan is in git history.

1. The design

  • Delete supplier is a red button in the Edit supplier sheet only; a new supplier has nothing to delete. It sits on the left of the footer, away from Save.
  • It asks first, through the existing useConfirm (danger). The message says how many expenses use the supplier, or "It is not used on any expense."
  • After deleting, the toast reads "Supplier deleted". The supplier leaves the list and the expense being edited goes back to "No supplier".
  • The database already clears the supplier from those expenses (on delete set null), so no data is lost and no migration is needed.

2. A gap this also closes

After a supplier is renamed today, the other record rows show the old name until the page reloads. Deleting would leave a deleted name showing the same way. To fix both at the root, the picker tells Profit & Loss when suppliers change, and the records reload. That is one callback passed from index.tsx through the form to the picker.

Save Inspection from Any Step

Status: built, waiting on your browser test  ·  Date: 2 November 2026

Your request: an inspection can only be saved on the last section, so recording something on an earlier screen means clicking Next, Next, Next.

1. What I found

  • You’re right: Save only appears on step 5 (InspectionForm.tsx:2477). This was deliberate, so that every save ended with a review.
  • The only required fields are hive, date and time, all on step 1, so nothing on steps 2 to 5 needs to hold a save back.
  • There is already a shortcut: once step 1 is valid, tapping 5 Review in the row at the top jumps there. That still takes an extra tap and an extra screen, and nothing on screen suggests it.
  • Saving already reads every step’s values, whichever step is open, so the save itself doesn’t need to change.

2. The change

Save Inspection appears on every step, next to Next. Review stays as an optional last step for anyone who wants to check before saving.

3. Two effects to be aware of

  • Values filled in for you get saved without being seen. A new inspection pre-fills Super Fullness and right-sized frames from the last visit, and weight from a connected scale. Saving from step 1 stores them on this inspection without you opening the step that shows them. This already happens if you jump to Review, but Review at least lists them. I think that’s acceptable, because they are the hive’s last known values.
  • A near-empty inspection is one tap away. If the hive is preselected, date and time fill in automatically, so step 1 is ready to save at once. It only saves on a deliberate tap, and the form already treats visit details alone as a valid inspection.

5. Review

What changed

Only InspectionForm.tsx changed in the app: Save is on every step, in the new layout and in green. The save logic is now its own function, and the Enter key behaves as before. The tests and docs were updated to match.

Addition beyond the plan

Voice notes: Save is now disabled while a voice note is recording or being transcribed. Save now sits on the same step as the microphone, so without this a tap could easily lose the note.

Invoice date starts with the expense date

Status: built, waiting for your tests  ·  Date: 15 November 2026  ·  Feature doc: docs/features/profit-loss-suppliers.md

Your request: on a new expense, Date should start with today, and Invoice date should start with that date too.

Checked: Date already starts with today on a new record (getCurrentDate() returns YYYY-MM-DD), and you confirmed this on your device. Nothing changes there. The invoice date plan is in git history (205312cf).

1. Behaviour

  • New expense: Invoice date starts as the same date as Date (today).
  • Change Date first: Invoice date follows it, as long as you have not set Invoice date yourself. For example, you enter last Tuesday's receipt and change Date, and Invoice date moves with it.
  • Once you change Invoice date yourself, it stays as you set it.
  • Editing an existing expense: nothing is filled in automatically. Its saved invoice date, or none, is shown as it is, so old records are never changed quietly.
  • You can still clear Invoice date to save without one.

Apiary Map Review

Status: all three batches built. Batches 1 and 2 are live; batch 3 and decision E are waiting on your phone test and test run  ·  Date: 13 November 2026

Your request: a full review of the apiary map from a usability point of view, a feature point of view, and any other general improvements.

Scope I assumed: the hive-layout map inside one apiary (2D map at /dashboard/apiaries/[id]/map and its 3D view), not the community map reviewed yesterday. The previous community map plan that lived in this file is still in git history.

1. Where the map stands today

The map is a to-scale rectangle of the apiary. The owner drags hives and benches onto a 0.25 m grid, turns them, sets the entrance and north, and can print the result. A 3D view shows the same layout read-only. Every change saves at once.

2.2 Usability on a phone

#Finding
U1The map is always at least 720 px wide, so it scrolls sideways on every phone, even for the default 5 m apiary. There is no zoom, no fit-to-screen and no full-screen view. The user never sees the whole apiary at once.
U2The controls come before the map. Up to nine setup controls (entrance, bench size, add bench, print, size, north) fill the first screen, though they are used once and then rarely.
U3Hive details open below the map, often off-screen, with no scroll to them. A tap on a hive can look as if nothing happened.
U4Dragging is the only way to place or move a hive, and on a phone it needs a 200 ms hold that the screen never mentions. A quick drag does nothing at all. This breaks the project rule that every gesture has a visible alternative.
U5The map is always in edit mode for the owner. A held finger at the apiary moves a hive, and the move saves at once with no undo.
U6Queenless is shown by a red border only, and there is no legend. An active hive with no queen recorded has the same green border as a healthy one.
U7View-only users cannot see unplaced hives, not even a count, and they still read the hint “Drag hives to match their real position”.
U8Loading is plain text instead of a skeleton; nothing shows that a change was saved; the “no hives yet” message has no link to add one.

2.3 Feature gaps

#Gap
G1The map shows almost none of what the hive list shows. Missing: inspection age, tasks due, treatment in the hive, stores level, super fullness (3D only), queen marking colour (fetched but unused). Two earlier feature documents already named the map as the place that “wants this most”.
G2No actions from a hive except “Open hive”. No “Start inspection”, no link to the queen. On a phone the apiary page hides its own “New Inspection” link, so the map is a dead end for field work.
G3Only the apiary owner can arrange the map. Team members of a shared apiary are always view-only. The feature document does not say so.
G4Nothing leads to the map. No “Show on map” from a hive, the hive list or a scanned QR tag; a new hive is not offered a place; the apiary page has no preview.
G5No offline view. Other field screens show a saved copy without signal; the map shows nothing, and a drag made without signal snaps back with a general message.
G6No landmarks. Sheds, trees, water, gates and text labels were left out of version 1 and never added. They are what makes a layout recognisable on site.
G7Mel cannot answer “where is hive 12?” No assistant tool reads the layout.
G8The print-out is the picture only, with no legend and no hive list.
G9Two position systems exist side by side. The older row and order fields are still on the hive form and have no link to the map.
G10Documents are out of date (version 1 map, 3D view and honey super documents), and there is no help page for the map.

3. Proposed work

Three batches, smallest and safest first. Each item is a small, separate change. Screen work follows the hivecraic-frontend-design and mobile-screen skills, and each batch ends with the QA audit and a documentation update.

Batch 1 — Faults and reliability

  • ☑ F1: a hive that changes apiary loses its map position, enforced in the database so every route is covered. One database change, applied after a backup. Existing phantom positions are checked and cleared.
  • ☑ F2: a failed load shows a message with a Try again button instead of an empty view-only map.
  • ☑ F3: super fullness is fetched only by the 3D view, and only the latest inspection per hive.
  • ☑ F4: the map reloads when the user returns to the screen, never while a save is in progress.
  • ☑ F5: follow-up saves treat zero rows saved as a failure.
  • ☑ F6: rollback snapshots are kept current, so a failed save restores the right position.
  • ☑ F7: “Delete bench” asks for confirmation through the shared confirm dialogue.
  • ☑ F8: “yard” replaced with “apiary” in three messages; titles in sentence case.
  • ☑ F9: 48 px buttons in 3D, Button component for “Open hive”, one shared “is placed” test.
  • ☑ QA audit and document update for batch 1.

Batch 2 — Phone usability

  • ☑ U1: the map opens fitted to the screen width, with Zoom in, Zoom out and Fit buttons. Hive tap areas stay at 48 px at every zoom.
  • ☑ U2 and U5: the map opens for viewing. An “Arrange” button turns on moving and reveals the setup controls; “Done” turns it off. (Decision B.)
  • ☑ U3: on a phone, hive and bench details open as a bottom sheet over the map.
  • ☑ U4: tap-to-place as the visible alternative to dragging: choose a hive, press “Move”, tap the new spot. The hint text explains press-and-hold.
  • ☑ U5: an “Undo” option on the message shown after a move, turn or removal (last change only).
  • ☑ U6: queenless hives carry a text or icon marker as well as the red border; a short legend sits under the map.
  • ☑ U7: view-only users see how many hives are not yet placed, and no dragging hint.
  • ☑ U8: skeleton while loading, a quiet “Saved” note after a change, an “Add hive” link in the empty state.
  • ☑ QA audit and document update for batch 2.

Batch 3 — Features

  • ☑ G1: a “Show” chooser that colours and badges the hives by one thing at a time: queens (default), inspection age, stores, tasks and treatments. The legend follows the choice.
  • ☑ G2: “Start inspection” as the main button in the hive details, and a link to the queen.
  • ☑ G3: team members with edit rights on a shared apiary can arrange hives. (Decision C; needs a check of all three permission gates.)
  • ☑ G4: “Show on map” from the hive page, opening the map with that hive selected.
  • ☑ G5: the map shows a saved copy without signal, read-only, with a clear “no signal” message on any attempt to change it.
  • ☑ G6: simple landmarks: shed, tree, water, gate and a text label. One new table. (Decision D.)
  • ☑ G7: a Mel tool that answers where a hive is and what stands next to it.
  • ☑ G8: the print-out gains the legend and a hive list.
  • ☑ G10: feature documents brought up to date and a help page written.
  • ☑ QA audit and document update for batch 3.

Not proposed

  • Background photo or satellite image, GPS alignment, custom apiary shapes, multi-select dragging, editing in 3D. Each is a large change for a feature that 4 accounts use today.
  • Live updates between two people editing at once. Reload on return (F4) covers the realistic case.

5. Review

13 November 2026. You accepted all recommendations (decisions A to F). Batches 1 and 2 are built. Type-check and lint are clean. No tests and no build were run. Nothing is committed.

Batch 1 — what changed

  • Database: a trigger on hives clears the map position when a hive changes apiary. It is live, and saved as supabase/migrations/20261113120000_clear_hive_map_position_on_apiary_change.sql.
  • Existing phantom positions were not cleared. A position left by an earlier move looks the same in the data as a real one, so there is no safe way to pick them out. No hive points at a bench in another apiary (checked: 0). If you see a hive at a wrong spot, take it off the map.
  • Loading: a failed first load shows a message with Try again (2D and 3D). The map refreshes on return to the screen, never during a save.
  • Saving: every write checks that a row was saved; a failed save puts back only what it changed; a bench is deleted only after its hives are safely on the ground.
  • Less data: super fullness is fetched by the 3D view only.
  • Small fixes: bench delete asks first; “yard” removed from three messages; page titles in sentence case; 48 px buttons in 3D; “Open hive” uses the Button component; one shared “is placed” test.

Batch 2 — what changed

  • View first: the map opens for viewing; the owner presses Arrange to move things and Done to finish. An empty map opens in arrange mode for the owner.
  • Fits the screen: the whole apiary fits the phone width, with Zoom out, Fit and Zoom in buttons.
  • Tap-to-place: tap a hive or bench, choose Move (or Place on map), tap the spot. Dragging still works, and the hint now mentions press and hold.
  • Undo: a “Hive 12 moved” message appears once the change is saved, with Undo. This also serves as the saved note.
  • Details panel: fixed above the bottom bar on a phone.
  • Legend under the map, and a red “!” on queenless hives.
  • Map setup (entrance, benches, size, north) moved below the map and shown only while arranging.
  • Other: skeleton while loading; view-only users see the unplaced count; an “Add a hive” button when the apiary has none.

Differences from the plan

  • The 15 degree turn buttons show no saved message, to avoid one message per press. The angle readout changes at once and a failure still shows a message.
  • Print is offered while viewing, not while arranging.
  • The “Apiary Map” pill on the apiary page keeps its title case, because every pill in that row uses it.
  • Decision E (map on by default) was done with batch 3, after your phone test.

  • An optional Invoice date field, for expenses only, next to Invoice number (the same two-column grid as Date and Amount). It starts empty: the existing Date stays the date the expense counts towards in totals, the chart and the year filter.
  • The record row adds the invoice date to the supplier line in the same date style as the row's date.
  • The CSV export gains an "Invoice date" column after "Invoice number".
  • Switching a new record to Income hides the field, and income saves it as empty, the same as the supplier and invoice number.

financial_records gains invoice_date date (nullable). No existing row changes. Applied through the MCP and saved as supabase/migrations/20261115190000_add_invoice_date_to_financial_records.sql.

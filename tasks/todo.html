Your request (with a screenshot of the top of hive card 29-DA): "See if that section on the Hive card can be rendered better. It's not looking great on the desktop, and I checked on the mobile, it's pretty much the same as way. It's not aligned and there seems to be space wasted."

The winter feed plan that was in this file is in git history (c6de020b).

1. Root cause

In src/components/hive/HiveListCard.tsx, the header is two flex columns side by side:

  • Left: the hive number, with the scale icons and QR tag, sharing, archived, tasks and treatment pills under it.
  • Right: a "status cluster" with the status pill, queenless pill, "Inspected N days ago" and the ⋮ menu. It wraps and is right-aligned.

The cards are only about 300px wide, on a phone and in the desktop grid alike, so the two columns fight for the width:

  • The right cluster wraps into three rows, which leaves ⋮ alone on its own row.
  • "Inspected 29 days ago" breaks over two lines.
  • The QR tag is cut to "HC-G…".
  • The space under "29-DA" is empty.

The comments in the code already describe working around this ("pushes these onto two or three rows").

2. The design

I'll split the header into two rows instead of two columns.

 Before                               After
 ┌──────────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────────┐
 │ 29-DA              [active]  │     │ 29-DA                    [⋮] │
 │ ⚖                ┌─────────┐ │     │ [active] [◷ Inspected 29 days│
 │ [HC-G…]          │◷ Inspec-│ │     │  ago] [▦ HC-GH7K2] ⚖         │
 │                  │ted 29 d…│ │     │                              │
 │                        [⋮]   │     │ [ Overview & Records       ] │
 │ [ Overview & Records       ] │     └──────────────────────────────┘
 └──────────────────────────────┘
  • Row 1: the hive number on the left takes the free width, and ⋮ is pinned top right where people expect a card menu. A long compound number still wraps, as it does now.
  • Row 2: one left-aligned row of pills that wraps naturally, in this order: status, queenless, Inspected N days ago, QR tag, scale icons, shared, archived, active tasks, treatment on. The most important state comes first. Every pill now gets the full card width, so the QR code shows in full.
  • Nothing else changes: the pill colours and wording, the menu contents, the Overview & Records button and the rest of the card all stay the same.

Checked against the house rules: no new cards, boxes, colours or motion. The menu stays the same button, and the text stays 14px.

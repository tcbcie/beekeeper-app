Show winter feed beside the stores gauge

Status: built, waiting for your tests  ·  Date: 16 November 2026  ·  Feature doc: docs/features/winter-stores-level.md (plan copy: docs/features/winter-feed-on-hive-card-plan.md)

Your request (with a screenshot of the hive card): "Beside the gauge where we report on the stores recorded, also show how much feed has been given to a particular hive in preparation of the winter. So that should probably be anything starting at the end of August for the current year."

The previous plan in this file (P&L list filter) is in git history (fb67afe4).

1. What exists

  • src/components/hive/StoresDial.tsx draws the gauge with two lines of text: "100% · Enough" and "Stores · Estimated 18 October". When there is no reading, it shows a grey arc and "Stores not checked".
  • HiveListCard.tsx shows the dial only in the stores season. storesSeasonStart() returns 1 August onwards and hides the dial from May to July. Archived hives show no dial.
  • useHivesList.ts already loads every feeding for the listed hives, but only hive_id, feed_date, which it uses for the "last record" row. Results are scoped by RLS, so team-mates' feeds count.
  • Feeds are stored in feedings with free-text feed_type, quantity and unit (L, kg or g; older rows may say "liters" or leave it blank).
  • Live data since 1 August 2026:
    • Syrup in three ratios: 116 rows, all in L.
    • Fondant in kg: 36 rows.
    • A few other rows: HiveAlive, pollen patty, and "frame of ivy honey" with no amount.
    • 40 rows have no quantity at all.
  • Mel's getRecentFeedings already applies the rule "only add amounts of the same feed and unit", using a private normaliseUnit.

2. The design

I'll add one extra line under the existing dial text, in the same column, so the stores level and the feed read together. No new card or box.

 ╭──╮   100% · Enough
 │ ╲│   Stores · Estimated 18 October
        Fed 5 times since 1 August
        12 L syrup · 2 kg fondant
  • Period. Feeds count from the same season start as the dial: 1 August this year (or last year when it is now January to April). One date rule then covers both the reading and the feed. See question A.
  • Amounts. All syrups (1:1, 3:2, 2:1, custom "… syrup") add up into one "syrup" figure in litres. Every other feed is totalled by its own name and unit, for example "2 kg Fondant" or "1 L HiveAlive". Amounts in different units are never added together.
  • Feeds with no amount still count in "Fed 5 times", so a frame of ivy honey isn't lost.
  • No feeds this season: the line reads "Not fed since 1 August". It also shows when stores were not checked.
  • The text is 14px tertiary, like the line above it. There are no new tap targets and no colour coding, because the feed isn't judged good or bad.
  • Out of scope: the compact phone list row (HiveListRow), the hive detail page and the map.

3. Decisions

  1. Start date: 1 August, matching the stores dial (your answer).
  2. Syrup: one syrup total for all ratios (your answer).

Notes

  • From January to April the line reads "since 1 August 2026", because the year is added when it is not the current year, the same as the dial's own date.
  • From May to July the line is hidden along with the dial, and archived hives don't show it either.

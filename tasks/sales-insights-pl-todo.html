Sales Insights and Profit & Loss: one source of truth

Review date 12/11/2026. Feature docs: docs/features/crm-module.md (Sales Insights section) and docs/features/profit-loss-tracker.md.

The question

Sales Insights (CRM, order management) does not show income recorded in the Profit & Loss tool. Do we need both? Should they be merged?

What the code does today

  Manual P&L entry ─────────────┐
                                 │
  Jar sale (Traceability) ───────┼──►  financial_records  ──►  Profit & Loss tool
                                 │      (the ledger)            income, expenses, net
  CRM order, fully paid ─────────┘
      │  (database posts one income row per category, tagged crm_order_id)
      │
      └──────────────────────────────────►  Sales Insights
                                             reads crm_orders ONLY
  • The ledger (financial_records) already holds every euro. When an order is fully paid, _crm_recognise_revenue posts its income into the ledger, and reverses it when the order is unpaid or cancelled. That part works. On your account all 9 paid orders (€889) are in the ledger.
  • Sales Insights reads orders only (src/app/dashboard/crm/insights/page.tsx:44-56). Jar sales and income you type into P&L never appear there. On your account that is 49 rows, €2,596.98, mostly Honey Sales.
  • The footnote is wrong for most users. It says the figures match "your income ledger" (insights/page.tsx:207-210). That is only true when every sale is an order.
  • Order income can drift in P&L. Rows posted by an order look like any other row in P&L (FinancialRecordCard.tsx) and can be edited or deleted there. The order still says paid, so the two screens disagree.
  • The two screens do different jobs. Sales Insights answers "who owes me, what must I fulfil, who buys most". P&L answers "what came in, what went out, am I making money". Only the revenue figure overlaps.

Recommendation: keep both screens, share one source of truth

A full merge (one "Finances" page) is not worth it. The CRM is a paid, opt-in module and P&L is in Tools for everyone, so a merged page would need two access levels. Its order-only parts (outstanding, to fulfil, top customers) mean nothing to someone who sells only at the gate. Instead:

  • The ledger is the one book of money. Every sales figure in Sales Insights comes from it, so jar sales and typed-in income appear, and the two screens can never disagree.
  • Sales Insights keeps the order desk parts (Outstanding, Open to fulfil, Top customers) and links to P&L for costs and profit.
  • P&L shows which rows came from an order and leaves those to the order, so nothing drifts.

Not in this change (worth knowing)

  • Double counting. Recording a jar sale for honey that was also sold through an order counts it twice. The app cannot tell, but the "orders / other" split makes it easy to spot.
  • Database lock on order rows. Edit and Delete are hidden for order rows, but RLS still lets the owner change their own rows through the API. A trigger could block that later if wanted.
  • Gaps. P&L has no category breakdown for people without the CRM, Purchase List prices do not post expenses, and Mel cannot answer money questions. Each could be its own small change later.

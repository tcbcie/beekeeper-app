Profit & Loss – year selector

Follows the P&L chart work (commit 55af9cc1). Feature doc: docs/features/profit-loss-tracker.md.

Why

P&L can show only this month, this year or all time. To look back at last season, for a tax return or to compare with this year, there is no way to choose 2025.

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • The period row becomes This Month | Year | All Time. "This Year" is renamed "Year".
  • When Year is chosen, a Year drop-down appears underneath, set to the current year. It lists the current year plus every year that has records, newest first.
  • Picking a year updates everything for that year: the totals, the chart (January to December of that year), income and expenses by category, the records list and the CSV export.
  • For a past year, the net line runs through all twelve months. For the current year it still stops at this month.
  • Panel titles name the year, for example "Income by category (2025)" and "Income and expenses by month (2025)". The CSV file is named pl-records-2025-….csv.

Batch 38: Queen Rearing

Status: Implemented 24/10/2026, waiting for your test. This is Phase 2 fix batch 25. It covers the open Medium findings in the Queen Tracker and mating nucs (C5a-06, 07, 12 to 17, 19, 20) and in batches and grafts (C5b-13, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20). Three agents checked each finding against the code and the live database (counts only), and I re-checked the key lines. No schema change; one optional data backfill (see the decision box).

A. Selection scoring ranks the wrong hives: C5b-16

Ratings never filled in count as 0: an unrated field is stored as 0. Of 1,891 inspections, 1,314 have no swarming rating, 830 no brood rating and 1,809 no chalkbrood rating. The averages count those zeros, so:
  • Swarming and chalkbrood: an unrated hive scores as perfect (6 − 0).
  • Brood: unrated inspections drag the average down.
  • Honey stores: left empty, they score the worst.
So the ranking mostly shows whose records are complete, not which hive is best. Now each average uses only the inspections that have a rating. A hive with no ratings at all for a field scores the middle of the scale (3), and the table shows "–" instead of a made-up number.
Every tap reloads everything: each weight button reloads every hive and all its inspections, and on quick taps an older answer can land last and show the wrong scores. Now the hives are loaded once per apiary, with only the latest load used. Weights and options just recalculate on screen. A failed load says so instead of "Not enough data".

B. Breeders and mothers: C5b-13, C5a-06, C5a-07

C5b-13, the breeder can be changed after cells exist: on a single-breeder batch, the "Breeder Queen" picker stays editable once cells are generated. Changing it re-parents every cell at once, including queens already in nucs, distributed or registered. It's now locked once cells exist, as the multi-breeder choices already are. New cells also record their breeder, as the multi-breeder feature doc says they should.
C5a-06, a breeder carried over from another cell: in the nuc form, picking a cell with a breeder fills "Grafted from". Switching to a cell without one left the old breeder in place. It now falls back to the batch's breeder, or clears. No live nuc is affected.
C5a-07, a nuc card with no breeder: the nuc card and its history only read the nuc's own breeder. Nuc Reports already falls back to the cell's breeder, then the batch's, so the two screens could disagree. The card now does the same. No live nuc is affected.
Decision: backfill the breeder on old single-breeder cells? 871 existing cells from single-breeder batches have no breeder recorded. Nothing is wrong on screen, because every screen falls back to the batch's breeder. A one-off update would stamp each with its batch's breeder, so old and new cells match and the data follows the feature doc. Done (you approved): 871 cells stamped; afterwards 0 missing and 0 disagreeing with their batch.

C. Loading and errors: C5b-15, C5a-12, C5a-15, C5a-17

C5b-15, stuck on "Loading grafts…": if loading the breeders, cells or nucs fails once, the batch editor shows "Loading grafts…" until you reload the page. Now it says "Couldn't load grafts" with a Try again button.
C5a-12, hidden sync errors: saving a nuc inspection reports "Inspection recorded" even when updating the nuc's or cell's status failed. The existing "nuc status could not be updated" warning now shows. Also:
  • A failed nuc list load says so instead of showing "no nucs".
  • A queen not marked as distributed after distributing from a nuc now shows an error.
  • Deleting an inspection no longer shows "deleted" straight after a failure warning.
C5a-15, cell list capped: the nuc form's cell list loads every cell in your account in one request. The largest account has 660; past 1,000 the high-numbered cells would quietly drop out. It now reads in pages.
C5a-17, tracker capped: the Queen Tracker's two lists aren't paged either, so past 1,000 rows the oldest queens would vanish. Today's largest is 36. They now read in pages, and errors show their real message.

D. Screens: C5a-19, C5a-20, C5b-19

C5a-19, 39 fields at 14px or under 48px: in the tracker, the nuc search, the distribute form, the nuc inspection panel, nuc reports, the batches filters and the cell editor. They become 16px and 48px tall.
C5a-20, the tracker table on a phone: it's 768px wide, so on a phone the Queen column scrolls away and you lose track of the row. Now:
  • The Queen column stays pinned while the rest scrolls.
  • The table is narrower on phones.
  • An opened row's details stay in view instead of needing sideways scrolling.
C5b-19, five hand-made pop-ups: "Assign cells to breeders", "Confirm Mating", "Distribute", "Nuc history" and "Change Mating Site" don't tell screen readers they are dialogs, don't keep focus inside, and most don't close with Escape. They move to the app's shared dialog. It also fixes a real bug: pressing Escape on Confirm Mating's own "are you sure?" closed both dialogs.

E. Closed without a change

C5a-13 (no try/catch): mostly not real. The tracker handlers and load are already wrapped, and Supabase reports errors rather than throwing. The one real case is the delete message, handled in C.
C5a-14 (stale fetch race): not real in the tracker or nuc code, which already has guards. The real race was in selection scoring, fixed in A.
C5a-16 / C5b-20 (select('*')): 11 uses, none broken. These tables have no column restrictions, unlike profiles, and they're narrow. Left as they are.
C5b-18 (database calls inside screen files): about 63 calls in 8 files. Moving them into hooks would be a large, risky change with nothing visible for you. Left as they are.

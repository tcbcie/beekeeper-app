Dashboard severe weather warning

Status: stage 1 built, approved by the owner with 90 km/h. Not yet tested in the browser or committed. Stage 2 not started.

Why

High winds have blown lids off and tumbled hives. A warning at the top of the dashboard gives time to strap and weigh hives down. The app has no warning today: no weather request asks for gusts, and only spring and swarm season fetch a forecast at all, so the windiest months have none.

Rules for stage 1

  • Looks at the next 72 hours, all year, for every apiary with coordinates and hives.
  • Amber from 90 km/h gusts, red from 110 km/h: Met Éireann's yellow and orange gust levels as reported in the Irish press. I could not confirm them on Met Éireann's own site.
  • Worded as a forecast, never "Status Orange", which belongs to Met Éireann.
  • Sits above the seasonal card. It cannot be dismissed, and it goes away once the wind has passed.
  • If the forecast fails, nothing shows, so no banner does not guarantee calm weather.

Review

Stage 1 adds a banner driven by one Open-Meteo gust request, with no database change. The audit found no critical or high issues. It fixed a title size that Tailwind would probably have overridden, and removed a screen reader wrapper that would not have announced anything. The banner only appears on a forecast of 90 km/h or more, so it has not been seen on a real page.

Profit & Loss – expenses by category

Follows the income breakdown (tasks/pl-income-by-category-todo.html, commit 620fed04). Feature doc: docs/features/profit-loss-tracker.md.

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • A panel Expenses by category sits directly under "Income by category". It follows the same time filter, for example "Expenses by category (this year)".
  • It uses the same bars. They are red, matching the Expenses card above, so income and expenses can be told apart at a glance.
  • It is hidden when the period has no expenses. There is no new query.
  • On a phone the two panels stack. On a wide screen they sit side by side, so the records list is not pushed far down.

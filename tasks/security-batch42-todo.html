Batch 42: Research Screens

Status: Implemented 25/10/2026, waiting for your test. This is Phase 2 fix batch 29, the second half of chunk 7b: the screen findings C7b-23 to C7b-25 in Research (GDD, Foraging Hours, Scale Overview, Diagnosis Images, Wild Colonies) and the tcbc.ie embed. The agent survey from batch 41 gives the exact list. No database change.

A. Tap targets: C7b-23

About 51 targets under 48px, 22 of them only 20–28px:
  • Small icon buttons: wild colony expand and edit; diagnosis view, download, delete and comment delete; and the "view hive" link on scale cards.
  • Small links: map, email and phone links; vegetation name links on the GDD tab and embed.
  • Embed controls: the year, period, month and temperature chips, and the Chart/Table and Accumulation/Phenology toggles.
  • Other: the "Add Records" links and the photo thumbnails.
They become 48px. Icon buttons keep their small look inside a 48px area. The diagnosis Send button also gets a label for screen readers.
15 fields under 48px or at 14px text, on the GDD, Foraging, Key Events, Diagnosis and Wild Colony forms, become 48px and 16px.

B. Text and charts: C7b-24

43 bits of readable text at 12px (weather and sensor values on scale cards, comment and table text, chart captions) go to 14px. 17 badges, table headers and captions stay at 12px, which the app allows for secondary labels.
8 charts: GDD accumulation, phenology and monthly temperatures; foraging accumulation and monthly; and the embed's three. Their labels are 9–12px and fixed to light-mode greys, so they're tiny and hard to read in dark mode. Changes:
  • Text: chart text, axis labels and value labels go to 14px.
  • Colours: they follow dark mode, the way the scale and TBR charts already do.
  • Small temperature charts: they get a little taller so the larger labels fit.
  • Phenology value labels: they hide when they would overlap.
  • Key-event labels: they're spaced further apart.

C. The wide wild-colonies table: C7b-25

The "All Colonies" table has 12 columns and runs off a phone screen. Its opened inspection panel is as wide as the table. On phones, 5 low-value columns (Submitted By, Size, Description, the two photo columns) are hidden, as is Type on the smallest screens. They stay reachable through Edit. The opened panel stays within the screen. The 9-column Pending table gets the same treatment for its least useful columns.
These are class and chart-option changes only: no change to data, saving or behaviour. Colours in charts update on the next redraw after you switch dark mode, like the existing charts.

Sales Insights – year selector

Matches the P&L year selector (tasks/pl-year-selector-todo.html, commit 45e71f42). Feature doc: docs/features/crm-module.md (Sales Insights).

What the page does today

  • Income in {year}, Income this month and Income by category: these read this year's income only. The query is limited to the current year (insights/page.tsx).
  • Outstanding and Open to fulfil: today's position across all live orders, with no year involved.
  • Top customers: the lifetime top five from the crm_customer_summary view. That view sums non-cancelled orders by order date, paid or not.

How it will work for the beekeeper

  • A Year drop-down sits under the page title, set to the current year. It lists the current year, the year on show and every year with income or orders, newest first. It uses the same SelectField as P&L.
  • Follow the chosen year: "Income in 2025" (with the orders and other split), Income by category (2025) and Top customers (2025, see the question).
  • "Income this month" shows only when the current year is chosen. A month card makes no sense for 2025.
  • Outstanding and Open to fulfil stay as today's position. They describe now, not a year. The footnote says so: "Outstanding and open orders are as of today."

Review (12/11/2026)

Plan approved with "Top customers follow the year". Built as planned in src/app/dashboard/crm/insights/page.tsx, plus the doc. No database changes. There is one query fewer: crm_customer_summary is no longer read.

  • Queries: the income query has no year limit. The orders query adds order_date, customer_id, customer:crm_customers(name).
  • Year: selectedYear state, with memoised yearOptions (the current year, the year on show, and the years of income and orders). The yearOf() helper reads the year from the date string.
  • Stats: income, the orders and other split, and categories use the chosen year's rows. Top customers comes from that year's non-cancelled orders. Outstanding and pending are unchanged.
  • Display: "Income this month" shows only for the current year. The headline row is three columns on desktop when it is hidden. Titles show the year, the empty texts name it, and the footnote says "Outstanding and open orders are as of today."

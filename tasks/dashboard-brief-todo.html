Dashboard brief: implementation plan

30 October 2026. Phase 1 built, with the owner's answers: keep a shorter seasonal card, narrow the queen check, correct the about page. Not yet tested in a browser or committed. Based on HiveCraic Dashboard Research and Concept, the screenshot inside it, and a review of the current code.

My view of the brief

The brief's central point is right: the dashboard should say what to do next and why, and never present a missing record as a problem. The current screen breaks that rule in a few specific places, and those fixes give the most value for the least work.

I would not rebuild the page. Much of what the brief asks for already exists, and some of it would undo what you decided this week. The plan keeps the seasonal card, fixes trust and layout first, then builds visit planning on the checklist you already have.

Risks

  • Depends on uncommitted work: the wind warning edits to page.tsx are not committed. They should be committed first so the two changes stay separate.
  • Reverses a recent choice: removing inLead means a hive getting lighter appears in both the card and To do. I chose that hiding, and you did not ask for it. Correct counts matter more than avoiding one repeat.
  • Evidence in the brief: its competitor review comes from product pages, as it says itself, and its review of HiveCraic comes from one screenshot. I checked the HiveCraic points against the code, but not the competitor claims.
  • Not browser-tested: all findings come from reading code and the database, so bug 5 in particular needs confirming.

Decisions for the owner

1. Seasonal card. I recommend keeping it, with shorter rows and one link per row. The alternative is the brief's plain list of items, which loses the seasonal question you liked.
2. Queen check rule. I recommend listing a hive only when it was actually inspected in the last 21 days and neither queen nor eggs were seen, or when it is recorded as broodless. Hives left closed then drop off the list. The cost: a hive nobody opens for months never gets this prompt, though in season the apiary-level Visit job still applies.
3. About page wording. I recommend "Install it on your phone; you need a connection to save records". Offline saving could be planned separately if you would rather make the claim true.

Review

Phase 1 is built. The database change is live, and the live function matches the migration file. The audit found no critical or high issues. Type and lint checks pass for the changed files. Nothing has been seen in a browser.

Differences from the plan: the varroa row shows a "Record treatment" link in place of the "Review" pill. Spring and swarm season keep their full-width button, as each has one clear next step.

Known limit: recent visits matches an apiary by name, so two apiaries with the same name would group together and link to the first. Fixing it means replacing get_recent_activity, which seemed too much for this edge case.

# HiveCraic - Business Description for Stripe

HiveCraic is a web-based beekeeping management platform serving hobby and commercial beekeepers across Ireland, helping them track apiaries, hives, inspections, and honey production through digital record-keeping tools. We offer annual subscriptions at €24 per year for general users, with a discounted rate of €12 per year for verified members of 79 Irish beekeeping associations across Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland. Users pay once per year via Stripe Checkout for 12 months of access, with no recurring billing or stored payment data on our servers. Our platform supports the Irish beekeeping community by providing affordable, GDPR-compliant farm management software while partnering with local beekeeping associations to encourage membership and best practices.
